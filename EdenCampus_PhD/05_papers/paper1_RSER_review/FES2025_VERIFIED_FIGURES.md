## Note on I&C flexibility (supports the paper's argument directly)

FES 2025 explicitly names industry and commercial as flexibility providers:
> "Industry and commercial can also offer flexibility in shifting non-time
> critical demand. Thermal storage could shift electrified high temperature and
> cooling demand, such as data centres, alongside growing flexibility from large
> refrigerators."

This is a direct, quotable FES basis for the paper's thesis that commercial
estates (business parks) are a recognised — if under-enumerated — flexibility source.
