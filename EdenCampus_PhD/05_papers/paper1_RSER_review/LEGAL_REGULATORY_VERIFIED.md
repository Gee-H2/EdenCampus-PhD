## Section 2 / 4 — Planning use classes & EU comparator

### ✅ England — Class E (2020)
**Claim (S2.2):** Class E "Commercial, Business and Service" introduced by the
Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2020,
effective 1 September 2020; consolidated former offices/R&D/light-industrial/
retail classes; permits movement between uses without change-of-use.
**Verified:** Correct. The Regulations (SI 2020/757) "come into force on 1st
September 2020"; Class E was introduced in Schedule 2 and "became operational on
1 September 2020." Former Classes A and D revoked and former B1 subsumed.
**Cite:** The Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) (Amendment) (England)
Regulations 2020, SI 2020/757 (legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/757).

### ⚠️ Scotland — Use Classes (the draft is now OUT OF DATE)
**Claim (S2.2):** Scotland retains Classes 4 (business), 5 (general industrial),
6 (storage or distribution) under the 1997 Order, "did not adopt the England
Class E consolidation, so the Scottish classes remain more disaggregated."
**Verified — partial correction required:** Classes **4, 5 and 6 remain valid**
and are unchanged (Class 4 Business, Class 5 General Industrial, Class 6 Storage
or Distribution) — so the business-park-relevant classes the review relies on are
still correct. **However**, the blanket statement that Scotland "did not adopt any
consolidation" is now outdated: the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted
Development and Use Classes) (Scotland) Miscellaneous Amendment Order 2023
(SSI 2023/35, in force **31 March 2023**) replaced Classes 1 (shops) and 2
(financial/professional services) with a new **Class 1A**. This does not touch
Classes 4/5/6, but the review must not imply the 1997 Order is wholly unamended.
**Action:** Keep the 4/5/6 point; add "(as amended; the 2023 Order (SSI 2023/35)
introduced Class 1A for retail/services but left Classes 4–6 unchanged)."
**Cite:** Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) (Scotland) Order 1997; as
amended by SSI 2023/35 (legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2023/35).

### ⚠️ Northern Ireland — confirm the year (2015 is correct, but note the history)
**Claim (S2.2):** NI operates "The Planning (Use Classes) Order (Northern
Ireland) 2015 — confirm year and relevant classes."
**Verified:** The Planning (Use Classes) Order (Northern Ireland) **2015**
exists and is the current instrument, made under the Planning Act (NI) 2011.
(Predecessors: 1973, 1989, 2004 Orders — do not cite those as current.)
**Action:** State "2015" with confidence; drop the `[VERIFY]`. Confirm the exact
class *numbers* you cite against the Order's Schedule if you name specific NI
classes (the review currently does not need to).
**Cite:** The Planning (Use Classes) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015, NISR 2015/XX
(legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015 — confirm the SR number on the Schedule page).

### ✅ RED II Article 22 — renewable energy communities
**Claim (S2.4):** RED II (Directive (EU) 2018/2001) Article 22 provides the
renewable-energy-community (REC) construct used as a legal comparator.
**Verified:** Correct. Article 22 of Directive (EU) 2018/2001 concerns
"renewable energy communities"; Article 22(4) specifically requires Member States
to provide an **enabling framework** to promote and facilitate their development.
Directive entered into force 24 December 2018.
**Cite:** Directive (EU) 2018/2001 of 11 December 2018 (recast), Art. 22
(OJ L 328, 21.12.2018) — eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2018/2001/oj.

## Section 6 — GB market & regulatory stack

### ✅ TCR — residual charges to fixed bands
**Claim (S6.1):** Ofgem's Targeted Charging Review moved residual network charges
to fixed bands; confirm effective dates.
**Verified:** Correct. TCR decision (Nov 2019) determined residual charges become
**fixed charges**, with **implementation from April 2022**.
**Cite:** Ofgem, *Targeted Charging Review: Decision and Impact Assessment*
(28 Nov 2019); implementation April 2022 (ofgem.gov.uk).

### ⚠️ P415 — status is now IMPLEMENTED (update the drafted "status" language)
**Claim (S6.3):** BM/wholesale access for aggregated flexibility via VLP under
P344, "with wholesale market access extended under P415 [VERIFY: P415 scope and
status]."
**Verified — update:** P415 ("Facilitating Access to Wholesale Markets for
Flexibility Dispatched by VLPs") was approved by Ofgem (Oct 2023) and
**implemented on 7 November 2024.** It introduced the **Virtual Trading Party
(VTP)** role (independent aggregators trading a customer's flexibility in the
wholesale market). Note: later modifications (P510, P511) are refining it as of
2026 — so describe P415 as "implemented Nov 2024, with subsequent refinements."
**Action:** Change "extended under P415" → "extended under P415 (implemented
7 November 2024), which introduced the Virtual Trading Party role."
**Cite:** Ofgem decision P415 (2023); Elexon implementation 7 Nov 2024.

### ⚠️ DFS year-round — confirmed, with exact date
**Claim (S6.3):** NESO's DFS "has been approved by Ofgem to operate year-round."
**Verified:** Correct. DFS became a **year-round service on 27 November 2024**,
moving to an in-merit margin tool. (A further evolved design was approved by
Ofgem in 2026.)
**Action:** State the date: "became a year-round service on 27 November 2024."
**Cite:** NESO, Demand Flexibility Service (DFS) service page; Ofgem Art.18
decision (Nov 2024).

### ⚠️ REMA — CORRECT the date/framing (Summer Update, July 2025)
**Claim (S6.4):** "REMA confirmed reformed national pricing in July 2025, with
zonal pricing rejected."
**Verified:** Substantively correct, with precise framing: in the **REMA Summer
Update (July 2025)**, DESNZ decided to **retain a single national GB-wide
wholesale market and not implement zonal pricing**, proceeding instead with
**Reformed National Pricing (RNP)** plus a reform package.
**Action:** Cite as "REMA Summer Update (July 2025)"; use the term **Reformed
National Pricing (RNP)**. Note nodal/LMP had already been rejected earlier (2024).
**Cite:** DESNZ, *Review of Electricity Market Arrangements (REMA): Summer Update
2025* (gov.uk, July 2025).
